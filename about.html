About EverGreen Tutor & Consult

EverGreen Tutor & Consult is an online learning platform created to help secondary school students learn with confidence.

Students can study Mathematics, English Language, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Computer Studies through lessons, quizzes, progress tracking, and a leaderboard.

Our goal is to make quality learning easy, engaging, and available to every student.
